# Gym Muse 🦆

A voice gym coach in one web page. It runs your workout plan, times your rest, counts your reps out loud and hands over to your training partner. No camera, no account, no backend.

**Open it:** https://ayushpareek493-tech.github.io/gym-muse/

On your phone, tap Share → **Add to Home Screen** to use it like an app. Your plans and history stay on your phone.

Plan format, one exercise per line:

```
Bench Press, 4 x 8, 60kg
Squat, 5x5, 100 kg, rest 150
Plank, 3 x 45s
```
