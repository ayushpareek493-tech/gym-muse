# Gym Muse 🦆

A voice gym coach in one web page. It runs your program, times your rest, counts your reps out loud and hands over to your training partner. No camera, no account, no backend.

**Open it:** https://ayushpareek493-tech.github.io/gym-muse/

On your phone, tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**. It installs like an app and works offline in the gym. Your plans and history stay on your phone.

## What it does
- **6-day program** (Push 1, Pull 1, Legs 1, Push 2, Pull 2, Legs 2). The app knows which day is next.
- **Voice coach** counts every rep, calls your rest and says what's next.
- **Heavy / lowered sets.** Lowered weight is prefilled at about 80% of your heavy set.
- **Rest-pause sets.** It counts your reps, talks you through 3 deep breaths, then "Go again!" until you can't move it.
- **Warm-up sets** suggested from your working weight.
- **Progression.** Hit the top of the rep range and it tells you to go up next time.
- **Progress page** with a chart, best lift and trend for every exercise. PRs are called out loud.
- **Training partners** (up to 3) take turns. Time-budget mode fits the workout into your minutes.

Plan format, one exercise per line:

```
Bench Press, 4 x 8, 60kg
Squat, 5x5, 100 kg, rest 150
Plank, 3 x 45s
```
